Help & support

Quick answers first. Still stuck? Email us and a real person replies, usually within 2 working days.

Contact support: support@kindose.app
Please don't send health details you'd rather keep private. We never need them to help.

Is Kindose medical advice?

No. Kindose helps you keep track of what your doctor prescribed. It never suggests or changes a dose. For a missed or late dose, check your medicine's leaflet or ask your doctor or pharmacist.

Where is my data kept?

On your phone. If you sign in, Kindose also keeps a private backup in your account so you can restore it on a new phone. See the privacy policy.

I got a new phone. How do I get my data back?

Install Kindose and sign in with the same Google or Apple account you used before. Kindose finds your backup and asks if you want to restore it.

Reminders don't arrive

Open Kindose → Me → Notifications. If it says notifications are off, tap the button to open your phone's settings and allow them. Also check quiet hours, battery saver, and Focus or Do Not Disturb modes.

How do I cancel Kindose Plus?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not by Kindose.

  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Kindose.
  • Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Kindose.

You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

I paid but Plus isn't on

Open Kindose → Me, tap the Kindose Plus card, then tap Restore at the bottom, using the same Apple ID or Google account you paid with. If it still doesn't work, email us with the date of purchase.

Refunds

Payments go through Apple or Google, so refunds are requested from them: reportaproblem.apple.com for iPhone, or Google Play → Order history for Android.

Export or delete my data

Me → Export my data shares your logs as CSV files. To delete your account and backup, see Delete account.
