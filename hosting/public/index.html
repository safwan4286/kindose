How it works

Five small things, done kindly.

1Log

One tap. Even when you're late.

Log your dose with the time, strength and spot. Took it last night and forgot? Pick the real date and time and your schedule stays right.

2Rotate

The right spot, every time.

Kindose remembers where you injected and suggests the spot that has rested longest. No more guessing.

3Nourish

Enough protein on low-appetite days.

Small, easy Next bite ideas help you reach your own protein and water goals, logged in a tap.

4Progress

Watch the change happen.

A clean weight trend with each dose change marked, so you can see what's working.

5Share

Walk in with a clear report.

A tidy PDF of doses, weight and side effects for your next appointment, ready in seconds.

Kindose is a tracker, not medical advice. It helps you keep track of what your doctor prescribed. It never suggests or changes a dose. For a missed or late dose, check your medicine's leaflet or ask your doctor or pharmacist.

Questions

Good to know

Weekly GLP-1 pens and vials, and daily GLP-1 medicines, including tablets. You pick your medicine and strength when you set up, and you can change it anytime.

So your plan and logs are backed up privately to your own account and you can restore them on a new phone. Your data still lives on your phone first, so logging works even without internet.

Kindose is free to download and everything is open for your first week. After that, Kindose Plus keeps logging and insights going. Your dose reminders, next spot and data export stay free. Plus is managed by Apple or Google and you can cancel anytime.
